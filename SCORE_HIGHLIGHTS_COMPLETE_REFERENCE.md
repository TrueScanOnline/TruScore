# Score Highlights - Complete Reference Document

**Purpose:** This document catalogs all possible Score Highlights that can be displayed when a product is scanned. It is intended to help create rules for which highlights can be displayed together and which ones should be excluded based on product characteristics.

**Last Updated:** 2024

---

## Overview

Score Highlights are displayed in the TruScore card and provide quick visual indicators (green flags for positive attributes, red flags for concerns) about a product. These highlights are generated by the `generateProductFlags()` function in `src/utils/productFlags.ts` and filtered through override rules in `src/config/scoreHighlightOverrides.ts`.

---

## Highlight Categories

Score Highlights are organized into 7 categories:
1. **Sustainability** - Environmental impact indicators
2. **Ethics** - Ethical certifications and animal welfare
3. **Nutrition** - Nutritional quality and health concerns
4. **Processing** - Food processing level and additives
5. **Geopolitics** - (Placeholder - not currently active)
6. **Boycott** - (Placeholder - not currently active)
7. **News** - Product recalls and safety warnings

## Complete List of All Possible Score Highlights

### 1. SUSTAINABILITY CATEGORY

#### 1.1 Excellent Eco-Score (GREEN)
- **Title:** "Excellent Eco-Score"
- **Type:** Green
- **Category:** sustainability
- **Severity:** high
- **Description:** "Eco-Score of [A/B] indicates minimal environmental impact"
- **Trigger Condition:** `ecoscore_grade === 'a' || ecoscore_grade === 'b'`
- **Data Source:** `product.ecoscore_grade`
- **Current Override Rules:** None

#### 1.2 Poor Eco-Score (RED)
- **Title:** "Poor Eco-Score"
- **Type:** Red
- **Category:** sustainability
- **Severity:** high (if grade E) or medium (if grade D)
- **Description:** "Eco-Score of [D/E] indicates significant environmental impact"
- **Trigger Condition:** `ecoscore_grade === 'e' || ecoscore_grade === 'd'`
- **Data Source:** `product.ecoscore_grade`
- **Current Override Rules:** None

#### 1.3 Palm Oil Free (GREEN)
- **Title:** "Palm Oil Free"
- **Type:** Green
- **Category:** sustainability
- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** "This product does not contain palm oil"
- **Trigger Condition:** `palmOilStatus.isPalmOilFree === true`
- **Data Source:** `product.palm_oil_analysis` (via `getPalmOilStatus()`)
- **Current Override Rules:** None

#### 1.4 Contains Non-Sustainable Palm Oil (RED)
- **Title:** "Contains Non-Sustainable Palm Oil"
- **Type:** Red
- **Category:** sustainability
- **Severity:** high
- **Description:** "Palm oil production is linked to deforestation and biodiversity loss"
- **Trigger Condition:** `palmOilStatus.isNonSustainable === true`
- **Data Source:** `product.palm_oil_analysis` (via `getPalmOilStatus()`)
- **Current Override Rules:** None

#### 1.5 Contains Palm Oil (RED)
- **Title:** "Contains Palm Oil"
- **Type:** Red
- **Category:** sustainability
- **Severity:** medium
- **Description:** "Palm oil production is linked to deforestation and biodiversity loss"
- **Trigger Condition:** `palmOilStatus.containsPalmOil === true && !palmOilStatus.isNonSustainable`
- **Data Source:** `product.palm_oil_analysis` (via `getPalmOilStatus()`)
- **Current Override Rules:** None

#### 1.6 Recyclable Packaging (GREEN)
- **Title:** "Recyclable Packaging"
- **Type:** Green
- **Category:** sustainability
- **Severity:** low
- **Description:** "Product packaging is recyclable"
- **Trigger Condition:** `product.packaging_data?.isRecyclable === true`
- **Data Source:** `product.packaging_data.isRecyclable`

#### 1.7 Low Carbon Footprint (GREEN)
- **Title:** "Low Carbon Footprint"

- **Category:** sustainability
- **Severity:** medium
- **Description:** "CO2e per kg: [X]kg - below average"
- **Trigger Condition:** `ecoscore_data.agribalyse.co2_total < 2`
- **Data Source:** `product.ecoscore_data.agribalyse.co2_total`

- **Data Source:** `product.ingredients_analysis_tags[]`

## Current Override Rules Summary

The following override rules are currently implemented in `src/config/scoreHighlightOverrides.ts`:

### Rule 0: No Additives when Ingredients Card Not Displayed
- **Priority:** 15 (highest)
- **Condition:** No valid ingredients_text available
- **Action:** Exclude "No Additives" highlight

### Rule 1: Alcohol Products
- **Priority:** 10
- **Condition:** Product contains alcohol (detected via nutriments, categories, or keywords)
- **Action:** Exclude "High Body Safety Score" and "No Additives" highlights

### Rule 2: Fish/Seafood Products
- **Priority:** 10
- **Condition:** Product categories/name/ingredients contain fish/seafood keywords
- **Action:** Exclude "Vegan Product" and "Vegetarian Product" highlights

### Rule 3: Meat Products
- **Priority:** 10
- **Condition:** Product categories/name/ingredients contain meat keywords
- **Action:** Exclude "Vegan Product" and "Vegetarian Product" highlights

### Rule 4: Dairy Products
- **Priority:** 10
- **Condition:** Product categories/name/ingredients contain dairy keywords
- **Action:** Exclude "Vegan Product" highlight

### Rule 5: Egg Products
- **Priority:** 10
- **Condition:** Product categories/name/ingredients contain egg keywords
- **Action:** Exclude "Vegan Product" highlight

## Known Issues & Contradictions

### Issue 1: Vegan Product with Animal Ingredients
- **Problem:** "Vegan Product" highlight can appear for products containing milk, eggs, meat, or fish
- **Current Solution:** Rules 2-5 exclude vegan/vegetarian highlights for animal-based products
- **Status:** Partially addressed - may need additional ingredient-based detection

### Issue 2: No Additives without Ingredients Data
- **Problem:** "No Additives" highlight can appear when ingredients are not available
- **Current Solution:** Rule 0 excludes this highlight when ingredients card is not displayed
- **Status:** Addressed

### Issue 3: High Body Safety Score for Alcohol
- **Problem:** "High Body Safety Score" can appear for alcoholic beverages
- **Current Solution:** Rule 1 excludes this highlight for alcohol products
- **Status:** Addressed

## Recommendations for Additional Rules

Based on the analysis, the following additional rules should be considered:

### Recommended Rule 6: Vegan/Vegetarian with Allergens
- **Condition:** Product has `allergens_tags` containing animal-based allergens (milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, etc.)
- **Action:** Exclude "Vegan Product" highlight
- **Rationale:** Products with animal allergens cannot be vegan

### Recommended Rule 7: Organic with Many Additives
- **Condition:** Product has "Organic" label AND `additives_tags.length > 5`
- **Action:** Consider excluding "Organic Certified" OR "Many Additives" (may need business logic decision)
- **Rationale:** Organic products typically have fewer additives

### Recommended Rule 8: Excellent Nutri-Score with High Sugar/Salt/Fat
- **Condition:** Product has Nutri-Score A/B AND has high sugar/salt/saturated fat
- **Action:** Consider flagging as potential contradiction (may need review)
- **Rationale:** Nutri-Score A/B should not typically have multiple high nutrient warnings

### Recommended Rule 9: Palm Oil Free with Palm Derivatives
- **Condition:** Product shows "Palm Oil Free" but ingredients contain palm derivatives
- **Action:** Exclude "Palm Oil Free" highlight
- **Rationale:** Palm derivatives indicate palm oil presence

### Recommended Rule 10: Recyclable Packaging with Non-Recyclable Materials
- **Condition:** Product has "Recyclable Packaging" but packaging_tags indicate non-recyclable materials
- **Action:** Review packaging data consistency
- **Rationale:** Ensure data accuracy

## Data Sources Reference

### Primary Data Sources
- `product.ecoscore_grade` - Eco-Score grade (a-e)
- `product.ecoscore_data` - Full Eco-Score data including Agribalyse
- `product.nutriscore_grade` - Nutri-Score grade (a-e)
- `product.nova_group` - NOVA processing group (1-4)
- `product.trust_score_breakdown` - TruScore pillar breakdown
- `product.nutrient_levels` - Nutrient level indicators (high/medium/low)
- `product.additives_tags` - Array of additive tags
- `product.allergens_tags` - Array of allergen tags
- `product.ingredients_analysis_tags` - Analysis tags from Open Food Facts
- `product.labels_tags` - Certification and label tags
- `product.certifications` - Array of certification objects
- `product.palm_oil_analysis` - Palm oil analysis object (from `getPalmOilStatus()`)
- `product.packaging_data` - Packaging information
- `product.recalls` - Array of recall objects

### Derived Data Sources
- `getPalmOilStatus(product.palm_oil_analysis)` - Palm oil status calculation
- `extractManufacturingCountry(product)` - Manufacturing country extraction

## Testing Recommendations

When creating new override rules, test with the following product types:

1. **Dairy Products:** Milk, cheese, yogurt, butter
2. **Meat Products:** Beef, pork, chicken, lamb
3. **Seafood:** Fish, tuna, salmon, shrimp
4. **Egg Products:** Whole eggs, egg-based products
5. **Alcohol:** Wine, beer, spirits
6. **Vegan Products:** Plant-based alternatives
7. **Organic Products:** Certified organic foods
8. **High-Additive Products:** Processed foods with many additives
9. **Products with Recalls:** Items with active recalls
10. **Products without Ingredients:** Items missing ingredient data

## Notes

- All highlights are case-insensitive when matching keywords
- Override rules are applied in priority order (higher priority first)
- Multiple rules can apply to the same product
- Rules are additive - if multiple rules exclude the same highlight, it will be excluded
- Placeholder categories (geopolitics, boycott) are not currently active but are included for future implementation
