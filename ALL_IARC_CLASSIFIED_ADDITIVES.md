## NOTES

**Substances NOT in E-number system but IARC-classified:**
- **Acrylamide** - Formed during cooking (not an additive)
- **Furan** - Formed during food processing (not an additive)
- **Aflatoxins** - Naturally occurring toxins (not an additive)

These are not food additives (E-numbers) but are formed during cooking/processing or occur naturally. They are not included in the additive database as they are not intentionally added to foods.
