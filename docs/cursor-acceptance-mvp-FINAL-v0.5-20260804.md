## 1. Acceptance

**Accepted.**

Cursor accepts **FINAL v0.5** as the founder-approved controlling MVP launch scope baseline for Australia & New Zealand.

Cursor will:

1. Treat FINAL v0.5 as the product-scope source of truth (outcomes, Post-MVP exclusions, waves, gates, roles).  
2. Continue **W0–W11 as-built / provenance** as **Wave 0** evidence only — **not** as a reason to reopen or renegotiate product scope.  
3. Adhere to the **closed-loop P0 protocol** for further scope feedback (below).  
4. Not infer unresolved product rules; not invent scoring effects for community contributions; not treat documentation gaps as automatic standalone-Spec tasks.  
5. Not expand scope, activate Post-MVP surfaces, or begin broad implementation merely because an item is MVP Required.

### Closed-loop P0 protocol (Cursor will adhere)

Further **scope** feedback from Cursor is raised **only** where Cursor identifies:

| Gate | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **P0 mission-critical contradiction** | A FINAL v0.5 requirement that conflicts with architecture or mission-critical safety in a way that cannot be delivered as stated without breaking the product foundation |
| **Material factual error** | A statement in FINAL v0.5 that is factually wrong about the product, constraints, or dependencies |
| **Unsafe without founder decision** | A requirement that cannot be implemented safely without an explicit founder decision |

**Out of scope for reopening FINAL v0.5:** preferred alternative designs, feature enhancements, sequencing preferences already decided, “would be nicer if…”, or ordinary implementation/Spec/UAT findings.

Subsequent findings are dispositioned as **implementation, specification, risk, UAT, or delivery** matters **against** FINAL v0.5 — they do **not** reopen the scope loop.
